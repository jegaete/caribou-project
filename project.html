Study Overview

CARIBOU Project – Integrated Care Pathway for Adolescent Depression

Background

Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) is a highly prevalent and disabling condition among adolescents, associated with significant impairment, increased risk of suicide, and long-term adverse outcomes. Although effective treatments exist, a large proportion of adolescents do not receive timely or evidence-based care, particularly in primary care settings.

In Chile, the prevalence of adolescent depression is estimated at approximately 8.3%, and a substantial treatment gap persists. Current care is often heterogeneous, lacks structured implementation, and does not consistently follow evidence-based guidelines.

Integrated Care Pathways (ICPs) have been proposed as a strategy to bridge the gap between clinical guidelines and real-world implementation by structuring multidisciplinary, measurement-based, and patient-centered care.

General Objective

To evaluate the acceptability, feasibility, and explore the efficacy of the CARIBOU Integrated Care Pathway in treating adolescent depression in primary care settings.

Specific Objectives

  • Assess acceptability among adolescents, caregivers, and healthcare professionals
  • Evaluate feasibility of implementing CARIBOU in primary care settings
  • Explore efficacy in reducing depressive symptoms compared to treatment as usual (TAU)
  • Assess impact on anxiety, quality of life, functioning, and family dynamics

Study Design

Phase 1: Formative Work

  • Translation and cultural adaptation of CARIBOU materials
  • Youth and family engagement using participatory methods

Phase 2: Intervention Evaluation

  • Pilot cluster Randomized Controlled Trial (cRCT)
  • 72 adolescents with depression
  • Randomization at the primary care center level
  • Comparison: CARIBOU vs Treatment As Usual (TAU)
  • Follow-up duration: 24 weeks

Setting

The study is conducted in primary care centers (CESFAMs) in the Metropolitan Region of Chile, including centers affiliated with the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile (ANCORA network).

Outcomes

Primary outcome

  • Depressive symptoms (Mood and Feelings Questionnaire)

Secondary outcomes

  • Anxiety symptoms (GAD-7)
  • Quality of life (KIDSCREEN-27)
  • Family functioning (MFAD-GF)
  • Global functioning (Columbia Impairment Scale)

Expected Impact

This project will provide critical evidence on the implementation and potential effectiveness of an Integrated Care Pathway for adolescent depression in a resource-constrained setting. Findings will inform clinical practice, health policy, and future large-scale trials aimed at improving mental health care for adolescents in Chile and Latin America.
