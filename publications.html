Suicide Prevention and Digital Interventions

This line of research focuses on the development, adaptation, and evaluation of evidence-based interventions aimed at reducing suicidal ideation among adolescents and young people. These studies include school-based and digital interventions, as well as adaptations across different populations and contexts, including vulnerable groups in Chile.

Together, these interventions aim to improve access to care, increase engagement, and provide scalable and effective strategies for suicide prevention across educational and clinical settings.

Mechanisms of Suicidal Ideation

This line of research focuses on understanding the psychological and behavioral mechanisms underlying suicidal ideation in adolescents. Using advanced analytical approaches such as network analysis, these studies aim to identify central processes that may serve as targets for prevention and intervention.

Villacura-Herrera C, Ávalos-Tejeda M, Gaete J, Robinson J, Núñez D. (2025). The underlying dynamics of a suicidal ideation latent network model: The role of hopelessness, psychopathology, emotion regulation, and behavioral coping skills in adolescents from the general population
Journal of Affective Disorders, 379, 540–548
