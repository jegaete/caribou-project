Principal Investigator

Jorge Gaete Olivares, MD, PhD
Universidad de los Andes, Chile

An adult psychiatrist trained at Universidad de Chile, with a PhD in psychiatric epidemiology from the University of Bristol. He studied medicine at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile and was a Clinical Research Fellow in child and adolescent psychiatry at King's College London. He is Full Professor at the School of Education of Universidad de los Andes and directs the Research Centre for Student Mental Health (ISME). His work focuses on mental health prevention for children and adolescents, particularly in school settings.
