CARIBOU Project

Evaluation of the acceptability, feasibility and exploratory efficacy of an Integrated Care Pathway for adolescent depression in primary care in Chile.

FONDECYT Regular Grant Nº1261819

Overview

Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) is a highly prevalent and disabling condition among adolescents, with significant impacts on functioning, development, and long-term outcomes. In Chile, a substantial proportion of adolescents with depression do not receive adequate or evidence-based treatment, particularly in primary care settings.

The CARIBOU project aims to evaluate the acceptability, feasibility, and explore the efficacy of an Integrated Care Pathway (ICP) designed to improve the treatment of adolescent depression within primary care centers.

What is CARIBOU?

CARIBOU (Care for Adolescents who Receive Information 'Bout Outcomes) is an Integrated Care Pathway that provides structured, multidisciplinary, and measurement-based care for adolescents with depression.

The intervention includes:

  • Structured clinical assessment and risk evaluation
  • Psychoeducation for adolescents and caregivers
  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (group and individual formats)
  • Caregiver support interventions
  • Evidence-based pharmacological treatment algorithms
  • Measurement-Based Care (MBC) with regular team reviews
  • Relapse prevention and continuity planning

Study Design

The project is conducted in two phases:

Phase 1: Formative Work

  • Translation and cultural adaptation of CARIBOU materials
  • Youth and family engagement to enhance relevance and usability

Phase 2: Intervention Evaluation

  • Pilot cluster Randomized Controlled Trial (cRCT)
  • 72 adolescents with depression recruited from primary care centers
  • Comparison between CARIBOU and Treatment As Usual (TAU)
  • Follow-up period: 24 weeks

Objectives

  • Assess acceptability among adolescents, caregivers, and clinicians
  • Evaluate feasibility of implementation in primary care
  • Explore efficacy in reducing depressive and anxiety symptoms
  • Assess impact on quality of life, functioning, and family dynamics

Setting

The study is conducted in primary care centers (CESFAMs) in the Metropolitan Region of Chile, including centers affiliated with the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile (ANCORA network).
