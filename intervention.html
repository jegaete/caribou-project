The CARIBOU Intervention

A structured, multidisciplinary and measurement-based care pathway for adolescents with depression.

What is CARIBOU?

CARIBOU stands for Care for Adolescents who Receive Information 'Bout Outcomes. It is an Integrated Care Pathway designed to organize evidence-based care for adolescents with depression through a structured sequence of assessment, psychoeducation, psychotherapy, caregiver support, medication options, and regular team reviews.

The pathway emphasizes collaboration between adolescents, caregivers, and clinical teams, while using repeated outcome measurement to guide treatment decisions over time.

Core Components

1. Identification and Assessment

Adolescents are assessed for depressive symptoms, diagnostic eligibility, functional impairment, suicide risk, and relevant clinical needs.

2. Mood Foundations

A psychoeducational session for adolescents and caregivers focused on depression, sleep, physical activity, healthy eating, and treatment expectations.

3. Psychotherapy

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy may be delivered in group or individual format. Brief Psychological Intervention may also be offered depending on clinical needs and engagement.

4. Caregiver Support

Caregivers may participate in sessions focused on validation, communication, boundaries, problem-solving, and supporting adolescents with depression.

5. Medication Stream

For moderate-to-severe depression, medication options may be considered using evidence-based algorithms and shared clinical decision-making.

6. Team Reviews

Every four weeks, adolescents complete outcome measures and meet with their care team to review progress and adjust the treatment plan.

7. Completion and Relapse Prevention

At the end of the pathway, adolescents and the care team discuss next steps and develop a transition or relapse prevention plan.

CBT Modules

The CARIBOU CBT component is organized into four skill-based modules:

  • Behavioural activation: increasing meaningful and mood-supporting activities.
  • Cognitive restructuring: identifying and working with unhelpful thinking patterns.
  • Communication skills: strengthening interpersonal effectiveness and relationships.
  • Problem-solving: developing structured strategies to address everyday challenges.

Measurement-Based Care

A central feature of CARIBOU is Measurement-Based Care. Adolescents complete repeated symptom and functioning measures during treatment. These data are reviewed with the clinical team to decide whether to continue, modify, intensify, or transition care.

Cultural Adaptation for Chile

In this project, CARIBOU materials will be translated and culturally adapted for implementation in Chilean primary care. Youth and family engagement will be used to improve language, examples, activities, and relevance for adolescents and caregivers in Chile.
